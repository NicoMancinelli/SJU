Hermex for Mac is a macOS client for [hermes-webui](https://github.com/nesquena/hermes-webui) servers, built as the Mac counterpart to [Hermex for iOS](https://github.com/uzairansaruzi/hermex) by Uzair Ansar (MIT License, © 2026 Uzair Ansar).
